Problem

Existing apps like Be My Eyes, Be My AI, and Seeing AI provide descriptions of environments, but where they struggle is precise guidance. Glance AI fixes that final stretch with intuitive vibrations to guide the user.

03

How it works

  1. 1

    Ask

    For a more personalized guidance, the user can ask Glance to complete a specific task or locate a specific object or ask for a more tailored description.

  2. 2

    See

    Glance gives simple instructions to the user. If needed, Glance might ask for more info or to look around.

  3. 3

    Walk

    Glance will adjust instructions live as the user is moving around. Glance will also interject with any hazards or dangers.

  4. 4

    Reach

    Haptic mode enables and Glance's glove gives intuitive 6 direction vibrations to guide the hand towards the object.
